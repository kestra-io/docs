Kestra Academy is the home for learning all things Kestra, with four courses to get you started and take Kestra into your own environment:

- 🧱 Kestra Fundamentals: the building blocks of a workflow, the syntax, and every part that makes Kestra tick
- 🏗️ Infrastructure Automation: run Kestra against your servers, clouds, and day-two operations
- 🤖 AI Workflows: orchestrate agents, models, and MCP tools with the same flows
- 📊 Data Pipelines: move, transform, and monitor data end to end

Every course ends with a certification you can share to show you are Kestra Certified.
